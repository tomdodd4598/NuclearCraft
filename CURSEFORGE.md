<p><span style="font-size: 14px;"><b><u>Features</u></b></span>
</p>
<ul><span style="font-size: 14px;">
<li>Forge Energy, RF, IC2 EU and GTCEu EU compatibility</li>
<li>Multiblock solid-fuel and molten salt fission reactors</li>
<li>Multiblock heat exchangers, condensers and turbines</li>
<li>Multiblock tokamak fusion reactors</li>
<li>In-depth nuclear fuel system featuring many fuel types</li>
<li>Complex fusion system of multiple fuel combinations</li>
<li>Full JEI compatibility</li>
<li>CraftTweaker recipe support</li>
<li>Complete Mekanism gas support</li>
<li>Fallout-style radiation system</li>
<li>More materials, weapons, tools and armour</li>
</span></ul>
